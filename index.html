What we do

Blasting and coating, start to finish

Most jobs come through the shop. Bigger or fixed structures, we bring the gear to you across South Canterbury.

Abrasive blasting

Grit blasting steel back to bare metal — the step most coating failures come down to. Structural steel, trailers, machinery frames, tanks and farm gear.

Water blasting

High-pressure wash-down of plant, tanks, concrete and building exteriors — salt, grime and loose coating off before anything gets painted.

Marine & industrial coatings

Epoxies, zinc primers and two-pack systems for gear that lives in salt air — hulls, deck fittings, port plant and anything the coast is working on.

Industrial spray painting

Steelwork and machinery spray finished under cover in the shop, so the coat goes on even and the cure isn’t at the mercy of a Timaru southerly.

Structural steel & plant

Beams, gantries, silos, conveyors and handrails blasted and re-coated on a maintenance cycle, so a line isn’t shut down twice for the same steel.

Rust removal & surface prep

Rusted, flaking or previously painted steel taken right back and checked over before the first coat goes anywhere near it.

About Blastcraft

A blast shop by the port, run by the two who do the work.

Blastcraft Limited has been registered and blasting in Timaru since December 1984. Andrew Larsen and Mark Johnson took the business over in 2021 and still run it hands-on — quoting the job, running the bay and putting the coating on.

Most of what comes through the shop is South Canterbury steel: farm gear, trailers, structural sections, plant and machinery, and anything that lives near salt air. If it’s too big or too fixed to move, the gear comes to you instead.

  • Locally owned and operated out of Timaru
  • Blast bay and paint shop, plus on-site crews
  • Quoted in writing before anything starts
